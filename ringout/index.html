Ringout — how to play

Two fighters, three stocks each. You don't win by draining a health bar: every hit raises your opponent's percent, and the higher the percent the further they fly. Knock them out past one of the four blast zones at the edges of the arena and they lose a stock. Last one standing wins the set.

Movement

ADwalk · hold to dash
←→same, arrow flavour
W / ↑ / Spacejump — tap for a short hop, hold for a full one. Press again in the air to double jump.
S / ↓crouch on the ground · fast fall in the air
S+Wdrop through a floating platform

Attacking

Jattack — tap alone for a jab
dir+J taptilt — quick forward / up / down pokes
dir+J holdsmash — charges while held, release to fire. Up to 1.5× damage and knockback.
J in the airaerials — neutral, forward, back, up, and a down spike
Kspecial — alone, or with a direction for side / up / down specials
Lgrab — then J to pummel, or a direction to throw

Defence

Shifthold to shield. It shrinks as it takes hits — let it break and you're stunned.
Shift+←/→roll — invincible through the middle
Shift+↓spot dodge — stay put, go briefly intangible
Shift in the airair dodge — directional, and it commits you

Off the edge

Fall past the stage and you'll auto-grab the ledge if you're close enough and falling slowly. From the ledge: W to climb up, J for a get-up attack, S to drop back off. Otherwise your up special is your recovery — NIX gets a long corkscrew, BRAM a short, brutal piston. Use it before you're too far down.

The roster

NIX
the Filament · light, fast, technical
  • Weight 78 — dies early, but two air jumps and the best recovery in the set.
  • Arc Bolt (neutral) — a fast little charge that skims forward.
  • Blink Slash (side) — phase forward through space, cutting on arrival.
  • Skyhook (up) — a multi-hit corkscrew that climbs a long way.
  • Phase Guard (down) — go intangible for a few frames; get hit during them and NIX answers back harder.
BRAM
the Bulwark · heavy, slow, devastating
  • Weight 122 — survives to absurd percents and launches you at half of yours.
  • Ironclad Fist (neutral) — hold to charge, armoured through light hits.
  • Bull Rush (side) — an armoured charge that eats a jab and keeps coming.
  • Piston Ascent (up) — a short, mean vertical launch.
  • Fault Line (down) — slam the floor and send a shockwave along it.
